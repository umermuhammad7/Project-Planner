The service

HomeThread helps households coordinate daily life. Features may include adult accounts, household creation and joining, child profiles, child device pairing, chores, rewards, events, calendar sync, meals, recipes, lists, push notifications, AI assistance, and subscriptions.

Eligibility and family use

HomeThread is intended for adults managing a household and for children using a paired child device experience under adult supervision. Adults must use their own Apple, Google, or email account. One adult should not share their account with another adult.

Children should not create adult accounts. A child profile and paired child device should be created and controlled by an adult household admin.

Accounts and security

  • Keep your sign-in account secure.
  • Use your own account, not another adult's account.
  • Control who receives adult invite codes and child pairing codes.
  • Tell Trendygrovee promptly about unauthorized access.

Households, invites, and roles

An adult can create a household and invite other adults. Each invited adult should sign in with their own account. Household admins may invite adults, add child profiles, pair child devices, manage roles, manage household content, and remove access where supported.

Members of the same household may see shared household content. Do not join or invite someone unless shared household access is intended.

Child device pairing

Child pairing codes are for pairing a child's device to an adult-managed child profile. They are not adult invite codes. Adults are responsible for supervising child device use, reviewing child profile content, and unpairing devices that should no longer have access.
