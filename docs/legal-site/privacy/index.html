Privacy Policy

HomeThread is a family coordination app operated by Trendygrovee. This policy explains what information HomeThread collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can request deletion.

Contact

Privacy and support email: trendygrovee@gmail.com

Information HomeThread collects

Adult account information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Authentication provider information from Apple, Google, or email sign-in.
  • Profile photo or avatar, if uploaded.
  • Locale, timezone, notification preferences, app settings, and push notification token if notifications are enabled.

Household information

  • Household name, household avatar, invite codes, and membership.
  • Adult member names, roles, colors, avatars, and joined dates.
  • Admin/member role information.
  • Subscription status, if paid features are enabled.

Child profile and child device information

  • Child profile name or nickname, avatar or color, role, and household membership.
  • Date of birth if an adult enters it.
  • Chores assigned to a child, completion history, stars, rewards, and notes.
  • Child pairing code status.
  • Child device label, device token, push token, paired date, last seen date, and unpair/revocation status.
  • Chore completion photos, if photo proof is enabled and uploaded.

Children should not create adult accounts. A child device is paired by an adult using a separate child pairing code controlled by the household admin.

Family content

  • Calendar events, event members, locations, reminders, recurrence rules, and countdown labels.
  • Chores, assignments, due times, recurrence rules, completions, notes, and photos.
  • Lists, grocery items, packing items, todo items, quantities, categories, and checked status.
  • Meal plans, recipes, ingredients, instructions, tags, notes, source URLs, nutrition fields, and favorite status.
  • Notifications created inside the app.
  • AI assistant conversation history and generated drafts, if AI assistance is used.

Optional calendar sync information

If calendar sync is enabled, HomeThread may collect calendar provider, external calendar ID, calendar access token, refresh token, token expiration, sync token, iCal URL, and imported event details.

Optional AI assistance information

If AI assistance is enabled, HomeThread may process user prompts and relevant household context such as member names, upcoming events, open chores, meals, lists, timezone, and dates. AI output may be saved as household content if the user chooses to save it.

Purchases, photos, diagnostics, and logs

  • If paid features are enabled, HomeThread may process subscription status and entitlement information through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • If photos are uploaded, HomeThread may store profile photos, avatars, recipe images, or chore completion photos.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure, HomeThread may collect crash reports, error diagnostics, app version, device type, operating system, server logs, request metadata, IP address for security/rate limiting, and related technical information.

How HomeThread uses information

  • To create and manage adult accounts.
  • To create, join, and manage households.
  • To let adults invite other adults with adult invite codes.
  • To let adults create child profiles and pair child devices with child pairing codes.
  • To sync chores, events, meals, lists, rewards, notifications, and related household content.
  • To send push notifications and reminders when enabled.
  • To import calendar events when calendar sync is enabled.
  • To provide AI assistance when enabled.
  • To process subscription entitlements when paid features are enabled.
  • To provide support, troubleshoot bugs, improve reliability, detect abuse, enforce rate limits, and secure the service.

How information is shared

HomeThread does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.

HomeThread may share information with service providers that help operate the app, including Supabase, Expo, Sentry, RevenueCat, Apple, Google, Google Calendar, OpenAI or Groq if AI features are enabled, and email providers if transactional email is enabled.

HomeThread may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or households, to investigate abuse or security issues, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Household visibility

HomeThread is built around shared household data. Adults in the same household may see shared chores, meals, lists, calendar events, child profiles, rewards, and activity assigned to household members. Do not invite someone to a household unless they should have access to that household information.

Children and families

HomeThread is designed for household use by adults and families. Adults control child profiles and child device pairing. Children should not create adult accounts or use adult sign-in flows.

Parents, guardians, or authorized household admins can request deletion of child profile data by contacting trendygrovee@gmail.com.

Permissions

  • Notifications are used for reminders, family activity, chores, events, and daily digests when enabled.
  • Photos/media access is used for avatars, profile photos, recipe images, or chore completion photos when a user chooses to upload media.
  • Calendar authorization is used only when a supported external calendar connection is enabled.

Retention and deletion

HomeThread keeps information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce terms.

Signed-in adults can request account deletion inside the app at Settings → Delete account. Adults can also contact trendygrovee@gmail.com.

Account deletion is intended to remove the adult's HomeThread user profile and adult membership links. Some shared household content may remain if it belongs to a household shared with other members, is needed for safety/security, or must be retained for legal, tax, audit, backup, or fraud prevention reasons.

Security

HomeThread uses technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated API access, server-side secrets, input validation, rate limiting for sensitive actions, database access controls, environment variables for secrets, logging, and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent, or request a copy of your information. To make a request, contact trendygrovee@gmail.com.

Changes

Trendygrovee may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, notice may be provided through the app, website, email, or another reasonable method.
