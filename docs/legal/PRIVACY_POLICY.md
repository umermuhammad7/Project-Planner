# HomeThread Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

HomeThread is a family coordination app for households. Adults can create or join a household, invite other adults, create child profiles, pair a child's device, manage chores, plans, meals, lists, reminders, and related family content.

This Privacy Policy explains what information HomeThread collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can request access, correction, or deletion.

HomeThread is operated by Trendygrovee (`we`, `us`, or `our`).

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When an adult signs in or creates an account, we may collect:

- Name or display name.
- Email address.
- Authentication provider information from Apple, Google, or email sign-in.
- Profile photo or avatar, if uploaded.
- Locale, timezone, notification preferences, and app settings.
- Push notification token, if notifications are enabled.

### Household Information

When adults use HomeThread, we may collect:

- Household name, household avatar, invite codes, and household membership.
- Adult member names, roles, colors, avatars, and joined dates.
- Admin/member role information.
- Household subscription status, if paid features are enabled.

### Child Profile And Child Device Information

HomeThread lets an adult create child profiles and pair a child's device. Depending on what the adult sets up, we may collect:

- Child profile name or nickname.
- Child avatar or profile color.
- Child date of birth, if entered.
- Child role and household membership.
- Chores assigned to the child.
- Chore completion history, stars, rewards, and notes.
- Child device pairing code status.
- Child device label, device token, push token, paired date, last seen date, and unpair/revocation status.
- Chore completion photos, if photo proof is enabled and uploaded.

Children should not create adult accounts. A child's device is paired by an adult using a child pairing code controlled by the household admin.

### Family Content

HomeThread stores the content that adults and paired child devices add to the household, including:

- Calendar events, event members, locations, reminders, recurrence rules, and countdown labels.
- Chores, assignments, due times, recurrence rules, completions, notes, and photos.
- Lists and list items such as grocery lists, packing lists, todo lists, quantities, categories, and checked status.
- Meal plans, recipes, ingredients, instructions, tags, notes, source URLs, nutrition fields, and favorite status.
- Notifications created inside the app.
- AI assistant conversation history and generated drafts, if AI assistance is used.

### Calendar Sync Information

If calendar sync is enabled, we may collect:

- Calendar provider.
- External calendar ID.
- Calendar access token, refresh token, token expiration, and sync token.
- iCal URL, if entered.
- Imported event titles, times, locations, recurrence information, and related metadata.

Google Calendar OAuth tokens are intended to be stored server-side and encrypted when token encryption is configured. Calendar sync should only request the scopes needed for the feature.

### AI Assistance Information

If AI assistance is enabled, HomeThread may process messages and household context needed to produce helpful drafts or suggestions. This may include:

- User-entered assistant prompts.
- Relevant household context such as member names, upcoming events, open chores, meals, lists, timezone, and dates.
- AI-generated replies and draft records.

Do not enter sensitive information into the AI assistant unless you are comfortable with it being processed for the feature.

### Purchases And Subscriptions

If HomeThread offers paid features, we may process subscription status and entitlement information through Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive full payment card numbers from Apple.

### Photos And Media

If you upload a profile photo, household avatar, recipe image, or chore completion photo, we may store that image and related metadata.

### Diagnostics, Logs, And Device Information

To keep the app reliable and secure, we may collect:

- Crash reports and error diagnostics.
- App version, device type, operating system, and technical metadata.
- Server logs such as request time, endpoint, error status, and security events.
- IP address where needed for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and logs.

HomeThread uses Sentry for crash/error monitoring when configured.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage adult accounts.
- Create, join, and manage households.
- Let adults invite other adults using adult invite codes.
- Let adults create child profiles and pair child devices using child pairing codes.
- Sync chores, events, meals, lists, rewards, notifications, and related household content.
- Send push notifications and reminders when enabled.
- Import calendar events when calendar sync is enabled.
- Provide AI assistance when enabled.
- Process subscription entitlements when paid features are enabled.
- Provide support, troubleshoot bugs, and improve reliability.
- Detect abuse, protect accounts, enforce rate limits, and secure the service.
- Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and App Store obligations.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate HomeThread, including:

- Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and related backend infrastructure.
- Expo for push notification delivery.
- Sentry for crash and error reporting.
- RevenueCat and Apple for subscription entitlement handling, if subscriptions are enabled.
- Google and Apple for sign-in, if those sign-in methods are used.
- Google Calendar for calendar sync, if a user connects Google Calendar.
- AI providers such as OpenAI or Groq for assistant features, if AI assistance is enabled.
- Email providers such as Resend for transactional emails, if email sending is enabled.

These providers are used to operate HomeThread and should be required to protect user data according to appropriate confidentiality, security, and data protection obligations.

We may also disclose information if required to:

- Comply with law, legal process, or government requests.
- Protect the rights, safety, and security of HomeThread, users, children, households, or others.
- Investigate abuse, fraud, security issues, or violations of our Terms.
- Transfer information as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

## 4. Household Visibility

HomeThread is built around shared household data. Adults in the same household may be able to see household content such as chores, meals, lists, calendar events, child profiles, rewards, and activity assigned to household members.

Admins may have additional controls, such as inviting adults, adding child profiles, pairing child devices, managing member roles, and removing access.

Do not add someone to a household unless they should be able to access that household's shared information.

## 5. Children And Families

HomeThread is designed for household use by adults and families. The primary account holders are adults. Adults control the creation of child profiles and child device pairing.

Children should not create adult accounts or use adult sign-in flows. If a child uses HomeThread, they should use a paired child device experience controlled by an adult in the household.

Parents or guardians can request deletion of child profile data by using in-app controls where available or contacting `trendygrovee@gmail.com`.

If we learn that a child has created an adult account without appropriate permission, we will take steps to delete or restrict that account.

## 6. Permissions

HomeThread may request permissions only when needed for a feature:

- Notifications: to send reminders, family activity updates, daily digests, and related alerts.
- Photos/media: to upload avatars, profile photos, recipe images, or chore completion photos.
- Calendar authorization: to connect supported external calendar providers when available.

You can change permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

## 7. Retention And Deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide HomeThread, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms.

Signed-in adults can request account deletion from inside the app:

Settings -> Delete account

Account deletion is intended to remove the adult's HomeThread user profile and adult membership links. Some household content may remain if it belongs to a household shared with other members, is needed for safety/security, or must be retained for legal, tax, audit, backup, or fraud prevention reasons.

Adults can also request deletion by contacting `trendygrovee@gmail.com` or visiting `https://vocal-malabi-9e6f2f.netlify.app/account-deletion/`.

Backups and logs may take additional time to expire from our systems.

## 8. Security

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account or household has been compromised, contact `trendygrovee@gmail.com`.
