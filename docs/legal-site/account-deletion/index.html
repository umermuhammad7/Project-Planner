Account deletion

Delete your HomeThread account

Signed-in adults can request account deletion from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, contact Trendygrovee by email.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open HomeThread.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the action if prompted.

If you cannot access the app

Email trendygrovee@gmail.com from the email address connected to your HomeThread account and ask for account deletion.

What deletion means

Deleting your account removes your HomeThread profile and adult membership access. Some shared household content may remain if it belongs to a household with other members, is needed for security, or must be retained for legal, tax, audit, backup, or fraud prevention reasons.

Child profile or child device deletion

Parents, guardians, or authorized household admins can request deletion of child profile or child device information by emailing trendygrovee@gmail.com. Include the household name, the adult account email, and the child profile name. We may ask you to verify that you are an authorized adult for that household.
